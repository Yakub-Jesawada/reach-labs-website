About

The REACH Lab (Robot Learning and Control Lab) is based in the Division of Engineering at NYU Abu Dhabi. Our research sits at the intersection of machine learning, control theory, and human-robot interaction — building robots that learn from experience and work safely alongside people.

We are part of the broader robotics ecosystem at NYU Abu Dhabi, collaborating with labs across the NYUAD engineering division and the global NYU network. Interested in joining? Reach out at reach-lab@nyu.edu.
